 Hi, I'm Ayaan, Team Underdogs.

 Quick question: when does the fastest lap LOSE you the race?
 When it's the wrong lap to spend your battery.

 We built Trackshift — a pit-wall co-pilot that knows when to push,
 when to wait — and when to say no.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
 [0:15] THE PROBLEM  (~25 s)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
 Deploy one hundred percent ERS, you get the overtake — and arrive at
 the final laps defenceless. Fast now, slow later. That's the paradox.

 And underneath it: Formula 1 does not broadcast battery state. Not
 on TV, not in the feed. Every pit-wall tool shows speed and RPM —
 none answer the only question that matters: SHOULD I PUSH NOW?

---------------------------------------------------------------------
 [0:40] THE SOLUTION  (~35 s)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
 Trackshift answers it in three layers.

 ONE — seven seasons of real F1 history: 571 sessions, both cars'
 actual laps in every duel.

 TWO — we simulate TWO cars at once. Leader and chaser. Attack AND
 defence. An overtake is a fight, not a forecast.

 THREE — we synthesize the missing battery, honestly: a published
 uncertainty band, plus or minus two to eight percent — never fake
 precision.

 On top: a decision engine scoring five strategies, with one hard
 rule — it NEVER recommends a move the battery can't pay for.
 It refuses. The refusal IS the product.
