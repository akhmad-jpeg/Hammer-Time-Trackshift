Synthetic battery-% tracking line — reconstructed within each lap from the telemetry samples: it drops as energy is deployed (full throttle) and rises as it is harvested (braking / lift-off). The battery starts the race FULL (100% = the 4 MJ usable Energy Store, so 1% = 0.04 MJ) and the opening laps burn it down into the working band — a full store cannot accept regeneration. The dashed 30% / 80% guides are a soft band, not a barrier: steady laps mostly cycle between them, but faster-than-average laps spend stored energy and can dip below 30%, while slower / management laps bank energy and can climb above 80%. Deploy / recover flow is modelled at up to 120 kW = 0.12 MJ/s. Per-lap totals from scripts/energy_simulator.py.

ⓘ What the call does

Projects THIS race forward from the current lap — no replay of a finished race. Each driver's career (aggregate) pace model predicts their lap time on the current circuit / tyre context; tyre ages tick up one lap at a time and the gap moves by the modeled pace edge. Once the chaser is within ~1.2 s the per-lap overtake probability is accumulated, and the call is the lap the cumulative chance crosses 50%. The chaser ERS sector sliders (S1/S2/S3, exactly the Energy Sandbox's vocabulary) shape the chaser's deployment per lap: each sector's delta is worth that sector's measured s/MJ, so a zero-sum shape can buy time by reallocating to the fast sectors without touching the battery; a positive net draws the 4.0 MJ store down to the 30% floor (faster), a negative net banks it (slower). Battery Now defaults to the mid-race working band (~62%) when left blank. The leader is assumed Balanced — a wall only controls its own car. No pit-stop or Safety-Car model — tyres run to the flag, so re-run after stops for the live picture.

ⓘ How the replay works

Uses the leader / chaser / track / year picks in RACE CALL above — resolves the two drivers' real race sessions for that track & season, then replays every shared lap to the flag. Tyre, fuel and ERS (race_state) features are read from the database, so energy_diff is real whenever the energy simulator has run for those sessions. Overtake % is split into S1/S2/S3 by where the chaser is gaining and where the braking evidence sits. Runs again automatically when you re-run the energy simulator for one of these sessions.

ENGINEERING DETAILS — ERS sweep & duel settings

Symmetric sweep re-scores each lap's energy_diff with that mode's projected trace on BOTH cars (only the ERS feature changes, so each row isolates that deployment choice). The asymmetric duel puts the leader on one ERS spec and the chaser on another (adds a LEADER > CHASER row/ring answering when the overtake happens when one attacks while the other defends).

ⓘ What this tunes

One-lap overtake probability and a short battle roll-forward for the leader/chaser scenario set in RACE CALL above (drivers, track, tyre state, current lap and gap). ⓘ What it compares

Compares Push / Balanced / Lift & Coast deployment over the laps remaining in that session. Battery "now" defaults to the stored energy trace at the chosen lap (full store if none).

SECTOR SANDBOX — PER-SECTOR DEPLOY DELTAS WITHIN ONE LAP
ⓘ What the sliders do

Per-sector deploy-delta sliders. The mode sets the lap's deploy envelope; deltas reallocate it between S1/S2/S3 (zero-sum). The 2026 PU has no fixed per-lap deploy quota — deployment is store-limited bursts (ceiling 8.5 MJ/lap). Watch for battery-floor and FIA-cap warnings.
