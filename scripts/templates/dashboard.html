Energy Management (synthetic ERS)
SIMULATE
SELECT A SESSION
Synthetic battery-% tracking line — reconstructed within each lap from the telemetry samples: it drops as energy is deployed (full throttle) and rises as it is harvested (braking / lift-off). The battery starts the race FULL (100% = the 4 MJ usable Energy Store, so 1% = 0.04 MJ) and the opening laps burn it down into the working band — a full store cannot accept regeneration. The dashed 30% / 80% guides are a soft band, not a barrier: steady laps mostly cycle between them, but faster-than-average laps spend stored energy and can dip below 30%, while slower / management laps bank energy and can climb above 80%. Deploy / recover flow is modelled at up to 120 kW = 0.12 MJ/s. Per-lap totals from scripts/energy_simulator.py.

RACE CALL (strategy co-pilot — one scenario in, one definitive call out)
0.8s
3 steps: ① click the round on the Calendar tab (preloads the pair + race) or pick them above · ② set how far the chaser is behind right now · ③ make the call. It replays the race from the current lap under Balanced / Push / Lift & Coast and answers with one call plus the alternatives — the end battery is always checked against the floor so the car is never left defenseless at the flag.
Run the call to get the strategy verdict — or click a round on the Calendar tab to preload the two cars and race.
Full-Race Breakdown — advanced (speed-trace replay · sector Overtake % · ERS duels · calibration)
Uses the leader / chaser / track / year picks above — resolves the two drivers' real race sessions for that track & season, then replays every shared lap to the flag. Tyre, fuel and ERS (race_state) features are read from the database, so energy_diff is real whenever the energy simulator has run for those sessions. Overtake % is split into S1/S2/S3 by where the chaser is gaining and where the braking evidence sits. Runs again automatically when you re-run the energy simulator for one of these sessions.
