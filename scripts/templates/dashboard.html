Energy Management (synthetic ERS)
SIMULATE
SELECT A SESSION
Synthetic battery-% tracking line — reconstructed within each lap from the telemetry samples: it drops as energy is deployed (full throttle) and rises as it is harvested (braking / lift-off). The battery starts the race FULL (100% = the 4 MJ usable Energy Store, so 1% = 0.04 MJ) and the opening laps burn it down into the working band — a full store cannot accept regeneration. The dashed 30% / 80% guides are a soft band, not a barrier: steady laps mostly cycle between them, but faster-than-average laps spend stored energy and can dip below 30%, while slower / management laps bank energy and can climb above 80%. Deploy / recover flow is modelled at up to 120 kW = 0.12 MJ/s. Per-lap totals from scripts/energy_simulator.py.
